# ATLAS paper review

1. Use the authorized owner history or a local signed PAPER snapshot. Check the source and timestamp. Treat missing evidence as unavailable.
2. Read the Council decision, the Senior Risk verdict and the settled result separately. Report exposure, drawdown and outcome in the units supplied by the ledger.
3. Read the relevant ATLAS method in `atlas-skills/`: market analysis, market structure, technical analysis, risk management or trade review.
4. Record review findings for the Council. Keep broker execution disabled and preserve the original ledger.
