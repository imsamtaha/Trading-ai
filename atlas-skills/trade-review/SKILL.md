# Trade Review

## Workflow
1. Inspect signal, technical, structure, risk, and execution state.
2. Identify the decisive acceptance/rejection factors.
3. Separate market-analysis quality from execution outcome.
4. Record lessons without changing safety thresholds automatically.

## Rules
Do not treat one profitable or losing paper trade as proof that a strategy is valid or invalid.
