## Data integrity and limitations

- This August 31 retrospective uses historical rows reviewed later. Revised prices and flows are not a point-in-time archive.
- The 34 daily candles support the displayed daily indicators. Weekly, 4H and 1H trend ratings are not assessed.
- RSI and ATR use 14-period simple averages. Wilder-smoothed platform readings may differ.
- No strategy backtest, win probability, calibrated conviction score or liquidation estimate is available.
- The $85,000 target is a scenario round-number target. The chosen swing's 1.618 extension is about $91,998.72.
- The supplied $99B ETF asset estimate lacks a valuation snapshot here. AUM changes include returns and net flows.

This package has no broker connection or order execution. Its optional public network scan is separately timestamped and does not modify the historical analysis.
