BEFORE YOU EXPLORE

Clarity starts here.

A few things to know about this preview.

Is this live market data?

No. The public demo uses fixed, synthetic examples. Every price and scenario is illustrative, with no claim about current market conditions or future performance.

Can ATLAS place trades from this website?

No. This public website is read-only and has no access to a brokerage account. The project's MT5 worker runs separately in a private Windows environment.

Does the demo require payment or registration?

No. Explore all three markets and scenarios freely. This preview does not collect account details or start a subscription.

Where is the subscriber dashboard?

The connected dashboard is a separate service that requires a configured data connection and access controls. This public preview does not provide access to private account telemetry.

Are these recommendations or return forecasts?

No. ATLAS provides decision-support context. These examples are educational and are not personalized investment advice or a promise of returns.
